Clover: Notes for Trilium

The iOS reading & writing client for TriliumNext — your self-hosted knowledge base on your phone, readable offline, fully searchable.

Coming soon to the App Store

This becomes the App Store button at launch.

Completely free — no in-app purchases, no ads, no data collection.

Designed for reading

Native typography with adjustable paper, font and size; continuous multi-chapter reading for book notes.

Full-text search

Works for any language, including CJK; filter by tag, type and time.

Offline first

Your notes are mirrored on the device; every write is queued, nothing is lost offline, conflicts never overwrite.

Writing too

Rich-text editor compatible with desktop Trilium, journal, share extension, voice input.

Your server, your data

Same login as the Trilium web UI with TOTP, SSO and Cloudflare Access; protected notes never touch the disk.

Works without a server

Built-in Local Vault: start on the device, upload to Trilium with one tap later.

Syncing requires a TriliumNext server (0.99+). iPhone and iPad. English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語.
